## The Moral Dimensions of an Internet Society 互联网社会的道德维度

Internet, like other technologies, can:

- Enable new crimes

  滋生新的犯罪

- Affect environment

  影响环境

- Threaten social values

  威胁社会价值

- Costs and benefits must be carefully considered, especially when there are no clear-cut legal or cultural guidelines

  必须谨慎权衡成本与效益，特别是在缺乏明确法律或文化指引的情况下。

- 互联网社会的道德问题可以从四个维度理解：Information Rights、Property Rights、Public Safety and Welfare、Governance。Information Rights 关注个人隐私、数据使用和知情权；Property Rights 关注版权、专利、商标和商业秘密；Public Safety and Welfare 关注互联网技术是否危害公共安全和社会福利；Governance 关注政府、企业和社会如何制定规则来管理数字技术。

### Privacy is about the information

- Privacy is a fundamental human right, but absolute privacy—where no personal data is ever exposed—is impossible.

  隐私是一项基本人权，但完全不被暴露任何个人数据的绝对隐私，是不可能的。

- At least mum knows her children.

- While the boundary could be very different under different social and technologies background.

- In a village, people know each other very much.

- In a city, people even has very limited information about their neighbors.


**Privacy is about the information**。隐私不是说一个人完全不被别人知道，而是关于个人信息被谁知道、知道多少、在什么范围内使用。

隐私是基本人权，但课件也明确指出：**absolute privacy is impossible**，也就是绝对隐私在任何社会中都不存在。比如在村庄社会，大家彼此熟悉；在城市中，人们对邻居了解较少；到了信息时代，互联网工具使得个人信息被收集和处理的能力远远超过过去。

### Big data

Internet tools enable the collecting and processing of personal information more powerful than any other time.

- **Digital Footprints**：Every online action leaves traces, making complete privacy impossible.

  **数字足迹 (Digital Footprints)**：互联网工具使个人信息的收集和处理能力达到了前所未有的高度，用户的每一次在线操作都会留下痕迹，使完全的隐私成为不可能。  

- **Surveillance Systems**– Governments and corporations monitor data, limiting true anonymity.

  监控系统——政府和企业持续监控数据，限制真正匿名的可能性。

- **Data Sharing**– Apps and services collect & sell user information, eroding confidentiality.

  数据共享——各类应用与服务收集并售卖用户信息，削弱了机密性保护。

- **Cybersecurity Risks**– Even encrypted data can be breached, exposing personal details.

  网络安全风险——即使是加密数据也可能被泄露，暴露个人详细信息。

- **Social Media Exposure** – Voluntary sharing reduces privacy, making secrecy relative.

  社交媒体曝光 – 自愿分享会降低隐私性，使得保密成为相对概念。

- **深度画像 (Profiling)**：通过追踪点击、搜索和浏览历史，零售商能够进行精准的用户画像分析。  

大数据在不同行业中的应用逻辑：  

- **犯罪预防 (Crime)**：大数据帮助执法部门洞察特定区域犯罪发生的原因，从而提供更有效的警务服务。  
- **医疗保健 (Health Care)**：应用于临床试验、预测流行病趋势以及优化患者护理管理。  
- **银行业务 (Banking)**：银行利用大数据分析识别异常消费行为，通过反欺诈技术保护账户安全。  
- **交通运输 (Transportation)**：GPS系统捕获其他驾驶员的实时数据，提供最新路况信息并重新规划路线以避开拥堵。  
- **在线购物 (Shopping)**：零售商根据用户的在线行为推送高度个性化的广告和内容，精准命中购买意向

两个具体的技术案例引发了关于“界限”的思考：  

- **iPhone 车祸检测 (Emergency SOS)**：
  - **正面**：在发生紧急情况且用户无响应时自动触发救援，能够拯救生命。  
  - **反面**：在过山车等极限运动场景中可能引发误报（Slide 10 的配图暗示了这一点）。  
- **人脸识别客流分析 (Facial Recognition)**：
  - **商业价值**：通过人脸识别进行精准客流统计、店员识别及画像分析。  
  - **风险**：大数据可能被用于实行“大数据杀熟”或“定制化价格”（Customized price），即根据用户画像对同一商品显示不同价格。  

大数据的核心伦理问题不是“大数据好不好”，而是 boundary 在哪里。它可以帮助救命，例如 iPhone 车祸检测可以在用户无响应时自动求救；也可以被滥用，例如人脸识别和用户画像可能被用于 customized price，也就是大数据杀熟。因此考试中可以从 benefit 和 risk 两个方面回答：benefit 是安全、效率、个性化服务；risk 是隐私侵犯、歧视性定价、过度监控和数据泄露。

## Intellectual Properties

- Intellectual property (IP) refers to creations of the mind, such as inventions; literary and artistic works; designs; and symbols, names and images used in commerce.

  知识产权（IP）指的是智力创造，例如发明、文学艺术作品、设计，以及用于商业中的符号、名称和图像。

- They can be classified to different types.

  - **Copyright** protects the original forms of expression but not the idea.

    版权保护的是原创的表达形式，而非创意本身。

  - **Patent** grants the owner an exclusive monopoly on the ideas.

    专利授予所有者对思想排他性垄断权。

  - **Trademark** used to identify and distinguish goods and indicate their source.

    商标用于识别和区分商品，并标明其来源。

  - **Trade secret** is secret, has commercial value, and has been protected by its owner.

    商业秘密是秘密的，具有商业价值，并且已获得其所有人的保护。

### Intellectual Properties Protection

- Intellectual property protection drives economic growth by **encouraging innovation** and **securing business value**.

  知识产权保护通过鼓励创新和保障商业价值来驱动经济增长。

- It offers a competitive edge, allowing companies to protect their market position and maximize the potential of their intellectual assets.

  它提供了一种竞争优势，使企业能够保护其市场地位并最大化其智力资产的潜力。

- Technology, healthcare, and entertainment heavily depend on IP to prevent unauthorized use and maintain leadership.

  科技、医疗与娱乐领域高度依赖知识产权以防未经授权使用，从而保持行业领先地位。

- Protection may have its time limitation. i.e. copyright and patent may expire under certain conditions.

  保护可能具有时效性。例如，版权和专利可能在特定条件下失效。

- Intellectual property protection is not absolute.

  知识产权保护并非绝对。在互联网环境下 IP 保护更加复杂
  
  - Internet greatly changes IP protection because digital contents can be copied and distributed much more easily than previous technologies. For example, music moved from physical records and tapes to CDs and online downloads, making copying cheaper and faster. This increases the difficulty of copyright protection.
  
    互联网极大地改变了知识产权保护，因为数字内容相比以往技术更容易被复制和传播。例如，音乐从实体唱片和磁带转向CD及网络下载，使得复制成本更低、速度更快。这增加了版权保护的难度。

支持产权保护的边界在哪里？IP保护并非绝对，存在“合理使用原则”（Fair Use Doctrine），如个人用途、评论与综述、司法程序、教育机构使用及讽刺作品等

关注 IP 的合理保护：

- 第一，用户可能在没有意识到的情况下侵犯知识产权；
- 第二，知识产权保护也可能被滥用。因此 IP protection 需要边界，不能无限扩大。

## Ethics and Law 伦理理论与法律监管

### Trolley Problem 电车难题

- **Utilitarianism** is a family of normative ethical theories that prescribe actions that maximize happiness and well-being for all affected individuals.
- Immanuel Kant 1724 – 1804：**Deontological** ethics or deontology is the normative ethical position that judges the morality of an action based on the action's adherence to a rule or rules.
- 自动驾驶场景说明，技术系统可能必须在极短时间内做出伦理选择。人类社会对极端场景没有预先定义好的完美答案，但 AI algorithm must make the choice。Law is always later than the scenarios，因此技术设计者不能只依赖法律，还要考虑伦理原则。

**代表性的西方伦理学派** 课件通过电车难题介绍了两种截然不同的西方伦理学视角：

- **功利主义（Utilitarianism）：** 以杰里米·边沁（Jeremy Bentham）为代表。该理论主张，正确的行为应当是能够**为所有受影响的个体实现最大化幸福和福祉**的行为（即追求利益最大化或损害最小化）。
- **义务论（Deontological ethics）：** 以伊曼努尔·康德（Immanuel Kant）为代表。这种立场认为，评判一个行为是否道德，应该基于该行为**是否遵守了既定的道德规则**，而不仅仅是看行为产生的结果。

**引入东方哲学思想** 在探讨解决方案时，课件不仅列举了西方理论，还结合了中国传统的哲学智慧：

- 道家的**“无为”**（可能暗示在极端困境下，不采取行动干预也是一种选择）。
- 儒家的**“齐身修家治国平天下”**（强调从个人修养到社会责任的递进）。

**在现代技术（人工智能）中的延伸**，将这一经典的伦理难题与数字时代的现实困境结合了起来，特别是自动驾驶汽车在遇到紧急避险情况时所面临的抉择。课件指出，人类对于这类极端情况并没有预先定义好的完美标准，但**人工智能算法却必须在瞬间做出选择（AI algorithm must make the choice!）**。

最后，**对于这些复杂的伦理问题，并没有简单的答案（No simple answer to the complex questions）**。法律和道德在面临类似困境时常常需要进行深度的权衡。

**Law 是底线，Ethics 是更高标准**。一个行为可能 legal，但不一定 ethical。课件用 “Do not be evil” 和搜索广告案例说明，企业不能只用“我没有违法”来证明自己没有作恶。Golden Rule 是：Do unto others as you would have them do unto you，也就是己所不欲，勿施于人。

### Digital Evidence 数字证据

Digital evidence is fragile and can be altered, damaged, or destroyed by improper handling or examination.

 **数字证据的脆弱性与收集规范** 在现代社会，我们大量依赖电子和数字证据。但**数字证据是非常脆弱的（Digital evidence is fragile）**，如果处理或检查不当，极其容易被篡改、损坏或销毁。

- 针对手机、硬盘等设备的数据提取，**只有获得授权的部门（Authorized department）才能进行数据收集**（例如使用Cellebrite等专业的数据提取工具）。

### Judicial Expertise 司法鉴定意见

- Judicial Expertise center is a lab with the judicial permissio

  司法鉴定中心是具备司法资质的实验室。

- Expert Report by Judicial Expertise can **increase** the creditable

  司法鉴定专家的报告能增加其可信度。

- **Cannot** work for the **disappeared** evidence

  无法为消失的证据工作

**提升证据可信度的三大途径** 为了证明数字证据未被篡改且具备法律效力，课件介绍了三种主要的司法与技术保障手段：

- **司法鉴定（Judicial Expertise）：** 司法鉴定中心是获得了司法许可的专业实验室。由他们出具的**专家鉴定意见书能够极大地增加证据的法律可信度（increase the creditable）**。当然，课件也引用了一个荒诞的现实反面案例（如“仅凭照片就对鹦鹉进行DNA鉴定”），以此反讽鉴定过程必须严谨客观。
- **时间戳权威认证（Timestamping Authority, TSA）：** 为了证明数据在提交前未被篡改，需要引入时间戳机制，且必须由**获得授权的TSA机构**来完成。这些时间戳服务器需要与导航卫星（GPS）的时间同步，通过数字签名和哈希算法将证据与准确的时间绑定在一起。
- **公证处保全（The notarial institute）：** 公证员在法律上被视为“特殊证人”，其出具的公证报告具有极强的法律效力。传统的公证保全要求**在公证处的全程监控下，使用公证处提供的电脑和网络环境进行操作和取证**。

### Preservation of Evidence 证据保护

- Blockchain is a hot technology.

- Organizations in the same chain can easily share the evidences.

- While, if one organization hold the chain, the credit cannot beyond the credit of that organization.

  如果一个组织掌控着链条，其信用无法超越该组织的信用级别。

**证据保全技术的未来演进** 传统的公证保全存在局限性，比如**人工服务无法满足7x24小时的取证需求**（例如要在半夜对欧洲足球赛事的盗播进行取证）。为了解决这一问题，课件介绍了两种前沿技术：

- **专用保全云（Special cloud for preservation purpose）：** 用户可以通过实名认证登录虚拟电脑环境（Portal），在云端使用屏幕录制、虚拟磁盘等工具进行取证，由公证云系统自动固定电子数据并出具电子公证书。- 
- **区块链技术（Blockchain）：** 课件指出区块链目前是一项热门技术，由于其去中心化和不可篡改的特性，**处于同一区块链网络中的机构可以非常便捷且安全地共享证据（Organizations in the same chain can easily share the evidences）**。

# 潜在考试题目与参考答案

## Q1. What are the moral dimensions of an Internet society?

答：The Internet can enable new crimes, affect the environment, and threaten social values. Therefore, its costs and benefits must be considered carefully, especially when there are no clear legal or cultural guidelines. The main moral dimensions include information rights, property rights, public safety and welfare, and governance. For example, information rights are related to privacy and personal data, while property rights are related to intellectual property.

------

## Q2. Why is absolute privacy impossible in an information society?

答：Absolute privacy is impossible because privacy is about information, and some information about a person is always known by others. In the information age, internet tools collect and process personal data more powerfully than before. Digital footprints, surveillance systems, data sharing, cybersecurity risks, and social media exposure all reduce privacy. Therefore, privacy becomes relative rather than absolute.

------

## Q3. Explain the benefits and risks of big data.

答：Big data can bring benefits in crime prevention, healthcare, banking, transportation and shopping. For example, banks can use big data to detect abnormal transactions and prevent fraud. GPS systems can use real-time driving data to avoid traffic jams. However, big data also creates risks, such as privacy invasion, surveillance, data leakage and customized pricing. Therefore, the key question is where the boundary should be.

------

## Q4. What is intellectual property? Give examples of different types.

答：Intellectual property refers to creations of the mind, such as inventions, literary and artistic works, designs, symbols, names and images used in commerce. The main types include copyright, patent, trademark and trade secret. Copyright protects original expression but not the idea itself. Patent gives the owner an exclusive monopoly on an invention. Trademark identifies and distinguishes goods. Trade secret must be secret, commercially valuable and protected by its owner.

------

## Q5. Why is intellectual property protection important?

答：Intellectual property protection encourages innovation and secures business value. It gives companies a competitive advantage and helps them protect their market position. Industries such as technology, healthcare and entertainment rely heavily on IP protection to prevent unauthorized use. However, IP protection is not absolute. Copyright and patents may expire, and fair use may allow some limited use for education, comments, reviews or parody.

------

## Q6. How has the Internet changed IP protection?

答：The Internet makes copying and distributing digital content much easier than before. For example, music used to be copied through physical media such as records, tapes or CDs, but now it can be downloaded and shared online very quickly. This makes copyright infringement easier and IP protection more difficult. At the same time, some people may violate IP rules without realizing it, such as using fonts for commercial purposes without permission.

------

## Q7. What is the difference between utilitarianism and deontological ethics?

答：Utilitarianism judges an action mainly by its consequences. It supports actions that maximize happiness and well-being for all affected individuals. Deontological ethics judges an action by whether it follows moral rules or duties. For example, in the trolley problem, utilitarianism may support sacrificing one person to save five, while deontology may argue that actively harming one person is morally wrong even if it saves more people.

------

## Q8. What is the relationship between law and ethics?

答：Law and ethics both guide human behavior, but they are different. Laws are rules created and enforced by social or governmental institutions. They tell us what we are expected to follow. Ethics suggests what we ought to follow. Law is usually the bottom line, while ethics is a higher moral standard. Therefore, a behavior can be legal but still unethical.

------

## Q9. Why is “Do not be evil” important in digital business?

答：“Do not be evil” means that companies should not only avoid breaking the law, but also consider whether their actions harm users or society. In digital business, companies may use algorithms, advertising and data collection to make profit. Even if some actions are technically legal, they may still be unethical, such as misleading search advertisements or abusing user data. Therefore, digital companies need both legal compliance and ethical responsibility.

------

## Q10. Why does the trolley problem matter for AI and autonomous driving?

答：The trolley problem matters because AI systems may need to make decisions in emergency situations. Humans do not have a perfect pre-defined answer for every extreme case, but an AI algorithm may still have to make a choice immediately. This shows that law is often later than new technology scenarios. Therefore, AI design needs ethical thinking, not only technical optimization.

------

## Q11. Why is evidence important in court?

答：Evidence is important because courts cannot directly see the past truth. They can only recover the truth based on records. However, a record is only part of the truth. A photo, video or screenshot may show only one angle or one moment. Therefore, the key question is how to make the court believe that the evidence is reliable, complete and not manipulated.

------

## Q12. Why is digital evidence fragile?

答：Digital evidence is fragile because it can be easily altered, damaged or destroyed by improper handling or examination. For example, files, screenshots, phone data and website records can be modified or deleted. If digital evidence is collected by an unqualified person or without proper procedure, its credibility may be challenged in court. Therefore, digital evidence should be collected and preserved carefully by authorized departments.

------

## Q13. What is judicial expertise and why is it useful?

答：Judicial expertise is an expert assessment made by a qualified judicial expertise center. Such a center is a lab with judicial permission. An expert report from it can increase the credibility of evidence. However, judicial expertise cannot work for evidence that has already disappeared. Therefore, evidence must be preserved in time before it is lost or changed.

------

## Q14. What is TSA in evidence preservation?

答：TSA means Timestamping Authority. It is used to prove that a piece of digital evidence existed at a specific time and has not been changed since then. The TSA should be an authorized organization. By combining trusted time, hash and digital signature, TSA can provide an official “birth time” for digital evidence.

------

## Q15. How can blockchain help preserve evidence? What is its limitation?

答：Blockchain can help preserve evidence because data recorded on a blockchain is difficult to tamper with, and organizations in the same chain can share evidence more easily. However, blockchain is not automatically trustworthy in every situation. If one organization controls the whole chain, then the credibility of the chain cannot be higher than the credibility of that organization itself.

------

## Q16. Compare judicial expertise, TSA, notarial preservation, special cloud and blockchain.

答：Judicial expertise increases credibility through expert reports from qualified labs. TSA proves the existence time of evidence through authorized timestamping. Notarial preservation uses a notary as a special witness to strengthen legal force. Special cloud allows users to collect evidence in a controlled virtual environment with screen recording and virtual disks. Blockchain helps organizations share tamper-resistant evidence. Each method improves credibility in a different way, but none of them can recover evidence that was never preserved or has already disappeared.
